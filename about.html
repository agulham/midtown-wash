Our Story

Two shops, one standard of care.

Midtown Smog has earned a 5.0★ rating across 60+ reviews on Yelp for fast, honest, no-pressure service. Midtown Wash was built to bring that same standard to car care — hand washing, detailing, and paint protection done right, without the chain-car-wash shortcuts.

We know Midtown drivers are busy. That's why we're set up so you can get your smog check done next door and leave with a spotless car in the same visit.
